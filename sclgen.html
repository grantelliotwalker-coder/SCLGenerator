How it works
  1. Describe your idea in plain words, or click an example idea. Add any manufacturer's info you have.
  2. Copy prompt and paste it into any AI chat. The AI is the middle man: it writes the FDS, pseudo code and block design and returns them as JSON in the exact format this page reads. It does not write the SCL.
  3. Copy the AI's reply and click Paste & go. The page builds the SCL block from the template rules and shows the FDS. If anything is wrong, Copy fix request, paste it into the same chat, and paste the new reply.
  4. Download the .scl (CRLF line endings for SIMATIC Manager or TIA) and Save FDS as PDF.

Everything runs in this page. Nothing is uploaded and it works with no internet connection. Your last spec is kept in this browser.

1Describe your idea

Try an idea:
What to include
  • Purpose: what the block controls or monitors, in one sentence.
  • Signals in: commands, feedbacks, measurements, with units and ranges.
  • Signals out: what it switches or reports.
  • Settings: setpoints, limits, start/stop levels the operator can change.
  • Timing: delays, confirm times, run-on times.
  • Faults: what counts as a fault, whether it latches, how it is reset.
  • Operator view: the states and messages the HMI should show.
  • Header: author and family names for the block, if you use them.
  • Datasheet: paste any manufacturer's info straight in here too.

Anything you leave out, the AI fills with a sensible default and lists as an ASSUMPTION in the block header.
