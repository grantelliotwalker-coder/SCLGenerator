How it works
  1. Click Copy AI instructions and paste them into any AI chat, online or local, together with the manufacturer's info. The AI writes only a JSON spec, not the SCL.
  2. Paste the spec into the left pane, or open or drop a .json file. The page checks it as you type. Click a problem to jump to it.
  3. When there are no errors, review the SCL and click Download .scl. The file uses CRLF line endings for SIMATIC Manager or TIA import.
  4. Compile the block in STEP 7, PCS 7 or TIA and test it. This page does not compile SCL.

Everything runs in this page. Nothing is uploaded and it works with no internet connection. Your last spec is kept in this browser.
